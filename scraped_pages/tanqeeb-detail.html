    Company Description

    Apexos Food & Beverage operates a multi-brand production facility specializing in the coffee industry. Currently managing two premium brands, Rafima Coffee Roasters and Mestila Coffee, the company is committed to delivering high-quality coffee products. As an emerging player in the field, Apexos emphasizes innovation, quality, and excellence across its offerings. The company's mission is to shape exceptional coffee experiences for its customers.

     

    Role Description

    This is a full-time on-site Marketing Executive role based in Heliopolis. The Marketing Executive will be responsible for developing and executing market plans, conducting market research, and identifying new opportunities for business growth. Key tasks include creating and implementing marketing strategies, working closely with sales teams and marketing agency to drive revenue, and building effective communication channels with clients. The role also involves analyzing market trends and competitor activities.
